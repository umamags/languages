Diacritic notes:
· ൊ / ോ — The short-o and long-o matras wrap around the left and right of the base consonant (split diacritics); rendering may vary by font.
· ൗ (au-length mark) vs ൌ (old-form au) — both are valid Unicode; modern usage prefers ൗ combined with the െ matra as ൌ.
· ം (anusvara / chandrabindu) — nasalises the syllable; pronounced like a nasal "m" or "ng" depending on the following consonant.
· ഃ (visarga) — adds a soft "h" echo after the vowel; mostly used in Sanskrit loanwords in Malayalam.
